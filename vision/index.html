Internal brief · the Tellera direction

The AI for what
AI doesn't tell you.

Tellera answers the real-world questions other AIs refuse — drawn from licensed public records rather than guesswork. This page explains what it is, the vision behind it, and how it builds on what LTV already owns.

What it is

One place to ask real-world questions — and get answers you can trust.

Tellera is a single "ask anything" assistant that answers questions about people, phones, addresses, property, vehicles, and court records. Unlike a generic chatbot, its answers are drawn from licensed public records — with a sense of how confident it is, and, where we can, a look at the sources behind them. You're not taking a black box's word for it.

"Who owns the house behind me?"
A generic AI declines — it has no access to property records. Tellera answers: Martin R. Decker, drawn from Travis County records, high confidence. Same question — a real answer instead of a shrug.
Why it exists

There's a whole class of questions AI won't touch.

People constantly ask practical, real-world questions — who's calling me, is this used car clean, who owns this property, are there liens on it, what's in this court file. General-purpose AIs either refuse these outright or guess and get them wrong, because they have no licensed data behind them. That's the gap Tellera fills.

A generic AI
  • "I can't access property ownership records."
  • No sources, or made-up ones.
  • No way to check its work.
Tellera
  • A plain-language answer from real records.
  • Backed by real, licensed records.
  • A confidence score, so you know how solid it is.
The platform

Nine specialists. One account, one login, one API.

Behind the single chat surface are nine focused agents — each an expert in one domain. You never have to pick one; an orchestrator routes every question to the right specialist(s) automatically.

Two ways in

A product for people — and a platform for builders.

The same engine powers two very different businesses.

For consumers
The Tellera app
A research assistant anyone can use — ask a question, get a straight answer. Simple, subscription-friendly, and trustworthy by design.
For developers
API & MCP
A REST API for any backend and a native MCP server so any AI agent can query real, licensed records instead of guessing. A whole new distribution and revenue channel for LTV's data.
How it fits LTV

This isn't new data. It's a new front door to the data LTV already owns.

Tellera isn't starting from scratch. It sits on 15 years of records infrastructure and the licensed data behind eleven trusted LTV consumer brands. Each brand becomes a specialist: BeenVerified powers people & phone, Bumper powers vehicles, Ownerly powers property, FamFinder powers family — and so on. One AI, unifying what today lives across many separate sites and subscriptions.

The vision

Own the AI transition — don't get disrupted by it.

The way people find real-world information is shifting from search boxes and subscriptions to asking an AI. Tellera is LTV's move to lead that shift on its own terms, using the one thing generic AI can't replicate: real, licensed data.

01
Trust is the moat
Anyone can wrap a chatbot. Almost no one can answer these questions from real, licensed records. That's LTV's edge — and it compounds.
02
One brand, one platform
Instead of many separate sites, a single AI that spans everything — simpler for customers, and a platform LTV can keep adding agents to.
03
Two revenue engines
A consumer subscription product and a developer data platform (API + MCP) — reaching demand the legacy model never could.
