Three tiers of sources

Not all data is equal. We label it.

Every field in a Tellera answer is tagged with the tier it came from. When sources disagree, the official record wins — and developers can require official-only data on any call.

Official
Government records
Pulled from the agency that keeps the record — county, state, and federal. The highest-confidence tier.
  • County recorders, assessors & courts
  • State registries, DMVs & unclaimed-property offices
  • Federal systems — PACER, NMVTIS, SEC, OFAC, Census
Partner
The LTV brand network
Fifteen years of linked data from BeenVerified, Bumper, Ownerly, FamFinder, ReversePhone and more — the connective tissue between raw records.
  • Identity & contact graphs
  • Valuation & vehicle-history models
  • Community reports & verifications
Vendor
Vetted third parties
Licensed suppliers that fill gaps government sources don't cover. Audited for accuracy and freshness before they reach an answer.
  • Carrier & portability data
  • Insurance total-loss & auction records
  • Listing history & adverse media

How we keep it right

From raw record to cited answer.

Public records are messy — misspelled names, duplicate parcels, stale addresses. Here's what happens before a record ever reaches an answer.

01
Collect
Scheduled pulls from thousands of sources — court dockets daily, tax rolls each cycle.
02
Normalize
Standardize names, dates, and addresses (USPS CASS) so records can be compared.
03
Link
Entity resolution joins records into one person, property, vehicle, or business.
04
Cross-check
Conflicts are resolved by tier and recency — the official record wins ties.
05
Score
Every field gets a confidence score based on source agreement and freshness.
06
Cite
Answers ship with source, tier, and as-of date — so you can check the work.
