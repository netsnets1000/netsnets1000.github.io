FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is there a free tier?
Yes — the Sandbox plan is free with 100 test calls a month and sandbox API keys. No card required to start.
How does overage work?
If you exceed your monthly included calls, extra calls bill at your plan's overage rate. Set spending caps and low-balance alerts in the portal.
Can I pay per call instead of subscribing?
Yes. Top up a credit balance and pay per call at the endpoint rate, with no monthly commitment.
What does an endpoint cost?
$0.01–$0.06 per call depending on the agent. The orchestrator (/ask) bills for the specialists it routes to on your behalf.
Do you gate sensitive data?
Court and people endpoints are permissible-purpose gated — you declare your use case per key, and we keep an audit trail.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. You keep access through the current period, then move to the free Sandbox tier — your keys and history stay intact.
